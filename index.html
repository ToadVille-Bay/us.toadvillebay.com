TBExit27 is now fully enacted

ToadVille Network LLC has fully been dissolved.

Overview

TBExit27 (ToadVille Bay Exit 2027) is an act that fully withdraw's ToadVille Bay's operation within the United States of America.

What will happen?

Services will remain active to U.S.A. users, however, ToadVille Bay will not have any servers within the United States of America, resulting in latency as you will be connected to foregin servers, either in Canada or Mexico, depending on your location, here is how TBExit27 affects you:

  • No regional pricing: ToadVille Bay Merch store will not be in USD.
  • Higher cost: Tariffs and international shipping can affect cost for you.
  • Limitations to services: No dedicated servers or entity can cause issues when it comes to support or connecting to ToadVille Bay.

What now?

You still can use ToadVille Bay as you would, but be aware that ToadVille Bay has no support services in the US. If you have questions, please email us!

support@toadvillebay.com
